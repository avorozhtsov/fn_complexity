Working draft · resource theory of maps

Exchange rates as quantitative structure on a hierarchy of maps

Artem Vorozhtsov · every numbered claim is marked proved or computed

Classifying a family of maps under an implementation relation produces a preorder and stops there. This adds the missing quantitative layer: an exchange rate counting how many copies of one map a second can supply per copy, and studies the geometry it induces. The main result is a structure theorem — every exchange geometry is a one-dimensional geometry plus a defect bounded by log 2, sharply, because the underlying Hilbert projective metric fibres over a line with fibres of diameter exactly one bit.

1

The framework is general, and it adds structure rather than objects

Fix any class of maps and any notion of implementation — a relation f ⪯ g meaning “g can be wired up to compute f”. Classification under ⪯ is the classical activity: it produces a preorder, and its Hasse diagram is the hierarchy. For polynomial maps over a field the standard choice is affine implementation, f = b ∘ g ∘ a.

A preorder answers one question — can g do f at all? — and is silent on the quantitative one. Writing f×k for the Cartesian power, block-diagonal composition gives k(r+s) ≥ k(r)+k(s), so Fekete’s lemma yields

C_aff(g→f) = lim k_{g→f}(r) / r(1) r→∞

It refines the hierarchy without changing it: f ⪯ g already implies C_aff ≥ 1, and C_aff grades every comparable pair by how many copies and every incomparable pair by a ratio the preorder cannot see. The construction needs only a monoidal product and an implementation relation, so it applies to any hierarchy of maps.

The computable proxy is the signature rate. The signature is the multiset of fiber cardinalities; fiber sizes multiply under Cartesian powers, and the entropy method gives

C(g→f) = inf log Z_g(β) / log Z_f(β), Z_a(β) = Σ a_i^β(2) 0≤β≤∞

Here β is an inverse temperature and Z a partition function with energy levels −log n_i. Equation (2) is a theorem about the operational limit, not a second definition.

The two rates are incomparable

Withdrawn

An earlier draft asserted that affine implementation forces signature implementation, hence C_aff ≤ C. That is false. Over F₃ take g = x²+y, f = x²; then f = g ∘ a with the singular processor a(x,y) = (x,0), so C_aff(g→f) = 1, while

C(σ(g) → σ(f)) = log 3 / log 6 = 0.6131471927654584… < 1(3)

The mechanism. Singular input processors are explicitly permitted. Here a is 3-to-1: it collapses the nine-point source onto a three-point line, and pulling back through it manufactures a fiber of size 6 from a map all of whose fibers have size 3. Signature implementation injects each target fiber into an assigned source fiber and cannot enlarge. Of the 25 ordered pairs of non-constant quadratic classes over F₃, nine have C_aff < C and four have C_aff > C.

Worse, C_aff is not a function of the signature at all: x and x²+y share (3,3,3), so their signature rates against x² agree to 40 digits, but C_aff(x → x²) = 0 and C_aff(x²+y → x²) = 1. No functor between the two processor categories exists in either direction. [affine_rate]

2

What the rate reads off an arithmetic family

Let f : A² → A¹ over F_q have geometrically irreducible fibers, N_c = #f⁻¹(c), a_c = q − N_c the Frobenius traces, m₂ = q⁻²Σa_c², and L = (q,…,q) the flat signature of a linear map.

Proposition 1 · proved

C(L→f) = log q / log max_c N_c, attained at β = ∞.

Proposition 2 · proved

1 − C(f→L) = (3−2√2)·m₂/(2q log q) + O(q^−3/2), with the infimum at β* = √2 − 1 = 0.414213562…, independent of family, genus and q.

So the two ends of the temperature axis read two classical statistics: the extreme Frobenius trace at β = ∞ and the second moment at β*. The interior carries the whole moment ladder, each order damped by ≈ 0.6/√q. Flatness is equivalent to a permutation-polynomial condition, which subsumes the known q ≡ 2 mod 3 result and reaches supersingular primes.

Two things are provably invisible, for structural reasons: the smallest fiber, because isolating it needs β < 0 where Z is no longer order-preserving; and the Katz–Sarnak symmetry type, because Σ a_c = 0 identically and that first moment is exactly the separating statistic.

The objection, stated plainly

C(L→f) is a strictly monotone function of max_c N_c, and 1 − C(f→L) is to leading order affine in m₂. The rate is a function of the signature and the signature is the list {N_c}, so nothing it reports could fail to be computable from {N_c} directly. Information flows into the matrix, not out of it. What survives is a statement about the comparison: of all ways to compress {N_c}, resource-theoretic comparison against a linear map returns exactly the extreme value and the second moment, at two specific temperatures.

3

Main result: one dimension plus a bounded defect

Write F_a = log Z_a, u_a = log F_a, R = log r, Λ = log(max fiber), σ = log(R/Λ), s = log β.

Theorem A · proved, verified to 3.6·10⁻¹⁵

u_a(e^s) = log Λ_a + max(σ_a, s) + w_a(s), 0 ≤ w_a ≤ log(1 + e^−|s−σ_a|)

with w_a unimodal, peaked exactly at s = σ_a, and 1-Lipschitz, of height ≤ log 2 with equality iff a is flat. Every profile is a kink plus one bump.

Splitting −log C into symmetric and antisymmetric parts — S = d/2 the metric, A the comparison — Theorem A gives

d(a,b) = |σ_a − σ_b| + ε, 0 ≤ ε ≤ log(1+e^−|Δσ|) ≤ log 2(4) A(a,b) = ψ(b) − ψ(a) + D, |D| ≤ ½ log(1+e^−|Δσ|) ≤ (log 2)/2(5)

Both constants are sharp and both bounds are independent of the number of points.

Theorem B · proved

d(a,b) is exactly the Hilbert projective metric between F_a and F_b — of the ambient cone of positive functions under the pointwise order, restricted to the achievable set, which is its projective closure. Cartesian powers are the projective rescaling the metric quotients out.

Theorem B′ · proved

log 2 is a projective diameter. With C₀ the σ = 0 fibre, diam C₀ = log 2 exactly, attained between max(1,β) and 1+β. The β-dilation group acts by isometries with geodesic orbits and every fibre is isometric to C₀.

The exchange geometry is the real line thickened by exactly one bit.

That is the geometric source of both sharp constants, and the attached Birkhoff ratio is tanh((log 2)/4) = 3 − 2√2 exactly. [realizability · birkhoff]

What Theorem B does not buy

No processor is a Birkhoff contraction. Tensoring is a translation Φ ↦ Φ + F_c, neither linear nor degree-1 homogeneous; every operation that is linear shifts σ by a constant, so its projective diameter is infinite and its ratio exactly 1, attained. Worse, tensoring expands d without bound — d(a, a^⊗k) = 0 while d(a⊗c, a^⊗k⊗c) → log k — so d is a pseudometric on resources and ⊗ does not descend to its quotient. The correct positive statement is that all four resource operations are nonexpansive in Thompson’s part metric.

Everything previously observed is a corollary

The ℓ₂-distortion ≈ 1.1 with c₂/log n falling — Bourgain’s O(log n) is unreachable because the non-line budget does not grow with n. Every negative-type violation confined to a window of diameter 2 log 2. The measured 4–9 % curl and its flatness in n from 8 to 698 points — the bounds in (5) are per-edge.

Corollary C · proved

The geometric mean of the rate asymmetry C(b→a)/C(a→b) around any preference cycle is at most 2.

Writing curl A = ½ log Ω with Ω the ratio of forward to backward arbitrage, the sharpness question is exactly “is Ω ≤ 2 — does forward arbitrage exceed backward by at most one bit?” The lower bound is proved in closed form and the supremum is not attained; the upper bound remains open between (log 2)/2 and 3(log 2)/2.

4

Are the cycles important?

The comparison is not transitive: 132 strict 3-cycles among genus-two pencils over F₁₁ in a complete enumeration, 1475 over F₁₃, with certified witnesses over F₁₁ and F₁₀₁. Three results weaken the obvious reading, one strengthens it, one bounds what can be claimed.

Against: cycles are not arithmetic

A random pool matched to a curve pool in the only things that matter has the same curl, and the tightest control has slightly more. Over 108 pools the curl fraction is a function of the trace spread to R² = 0.93. Cycles are a property of near-flat signature shape. [flux_arithmetic]

Against: cycles mostly do not descend to the maps

Since C_aff is not a signature invariant, the question is only well posed after choosing affine orbits. The seven signature cycles lift to twelve orbit triples, and eight of the twelve are refuted under C_aff. [affine_rate]

For — and this replaces an argument we had wrong

Withdrawn

An earlier draft claimed cycles are a zero-temperature artefact — that averaging over temperature destroys them. That is false. There are certified 3-cycles of flat signatures, which Theorem A proves cannot cycle at λ = ∞, living in bounded bands including one at λ = 1 exactly.

With c_β(a→b) = F_a(β)/F_b(β) the fixed-β rate and M_λ the power mean over a temperature prior, the whole comparison is

A_λ(a,b) = ½ log [ C_λ(a→b) / C_λ(b→a) ], C_λ = M_−λ( c_·(a→b) )(6)

λ = ∞ is the framework’s infimum, λ = 1 the plain arithmetic average of transition rates, λ = 0 the geometric mean. At a single temperature the comparison is a total order, since c_β(a→b)·c_β(b→a) = 1 — no pool cycles at any one temperature. The comparison is a potential iff λ = 0, the unique power mean commuting with reciprocals; but the same identity sends every distance to zero, so that scalar complexity is the potential of an empty theory. [maslov]

Non-scalarity is not an artefact of the infimum. It is a consequence of irreversibility — the infimum is the extreme case, not the source.

Where the cycles come from

Brandão–Horodecki–Ng–Oppenheim–Wehner govern conversion by a continuum of Rényi α-free energies — the same β-indexed family. Their scalar is an infimum of a difference, hence a partial order, acyclic by construction; ours is an infimum of a ratio, hence a tournament, which can cycle. And the ratio is forced: these resources are unnormalised, so the difference diverges like β(Λ_a − Λ_b). Cycles are the price of comparing resources with no common normalisation — the situation for maps. [quantum]

The hedge, and it is the number to quote

A single unfitted temperature prior reproduces 99.39 % of the tropical order on F₁₁ and 99.51 % on F₁₃; a fitted prior reaches 99.62 % and transfers across q without loss, against a ceiling of 99.90 %. Both beat the HodgeRank potential at 98.31 %. The irreducibly non-scalar part is about four parts in a thousand.

The defensible claim

Asymptotic convertibility is governed by no scalar complexity, because the comparison is a non-reversible aggregation over temperature. The failure is real but small: a single prior recovers 99.4 % of the order, and rates around any preference cycle differ by a geometric-mean factor of at most two. What this does not support is that cycles reveal something arithmetic. They do not.

5

Status

Proved and independently re-verified: Propositions 1–2, Theorems A–B′, Corollary C, the power-mean identity (6) and its λ = 0 reversibility, the flatness theorem, the two invisibility results, superadditivity of C and C_aff — and for C_aff, strict superadditivity, witnessed by x² ⊗ x → x²+y over F₃ with a gap of at least 1/2. One affine processor draws a degree-2 part from x² and a linear part from x, which neither factor supplies alone.

Exact affine rates

1/2 for x²→x, x²→xy, x²→x²+y²; 2/3 for x²+y²→x. In the last, the Fekete supremum is attained at no r ≤ 2.

Open and load-bearing

Prove Ω ≤ 2; whether C_aff cycles on affine orbits; whether any λ < ∞ is operational.

Three claims withdrawn

That the exchange metrics do not fill MET — C₄ is realisable exactly, and the obstruction rested on a stalled search. That C_aff ≤ C — false, with the singular-processor counterexample in (3). And that cycles are a zero-temperature artefact — false, with certified cycles at λ = 1. Each was refuted by the session sent to verify it.
