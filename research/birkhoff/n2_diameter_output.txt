=== (5) why Delta must be the diameter of T(K), not of T(domain) ===
  If the domain-restricted form were valid one could take K' = C_W =
  {Phi in C : |sigma| <= W}, a subcone of finite d-diameter
  <= 2W + log 2, and T = identity, whose Lipschitz constant is 1 but
  whose 'tanh(diam/4)' would be < 1.  Explicitly, with W = 0:
    diam C_0 = 0.693147180560,  tanh(diam/4) = 0.171572875254,
    but d(Id Phi_min, Id Phi_max)/d(Phi_min,Phi_max) = 1.
  So Birkhoff's Delta is the diameter of the image of the WHOLE cone
  whose order defines the metric.  (This is the step brief N warned
  was easy to wave at.)

=== (6) a positive linear map with image in C_0 really does contract by tanh((log 2)/4) = 3 - 2 sqrt 2 = 0.17157... ===
  T Phi = Phi(b1) * max(1,beta) + Phi(b2) * (1+beta):  R(T Phi) = Lam(T Phi)
  for every Phi, so T(K) is contained in C_0 and Delta(T) <= log 2.
  Delta(T) computed = 0.693147180560   (log 2 = 0.693147180560)
  Birkhoff ratio tanh(Delta/4) = 0.171572875254
  empirical Lipschitz ratio over 100000 random pairs of C: 0.140033465671   (<= tanh(log2/4) = 0.171573 required)
